📘 Companion to Why Waitlists Need to Be Longer Than You Think (Hajji-Fakir, 2025). The exact rule is Equation (4.1); the naive rule checks the wrong event.
Equation (4.1) — exact residual deficit risk P(R_w > 0) = Σ_{k=j_min}^{P-1} P(K=k) · F_{N-m, E-k, w}(P-k-1)
The simulator computes this sum directly. The naive rule replaces F_{N-m,E-k,w}(P-k-1) by the indicator 1{P-k > w}.

          Noncentral hypergeometric — §10 When merit and eligibility are correlated with odds ratio ω ≠ 1, K ~ NCHG(N, E, m, ω) with pmf P(K=k) ∝ C(E,k)·C(N−E,m−k)·ω^k. Set the ω parameter above to explore this regime. The conditional law of K_w | k is unaffected by ω, so Equation (4.1) remains valid with the hypergeometric pmf of K replaced by the noncentral version.
