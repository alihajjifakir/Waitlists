🔵 Why holes matter for the waitlist

Suppose the shortlist has U non-eligible candidates. These U candidates create U holes when removed. Each hole above the P-th rank means one additional eligible candidate must be pulled from the waitlist to fill the P positions. That is precisely why the naive rule P(Δ > w) ≤ α under-sizes: it counts holes as if they were automatically filled by eligible candidates.

U = m − K · # holes above P = max(0, P − K) = Δ · residual deficit Rw = max(0, Δ − Kw)
